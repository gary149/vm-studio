The 10 test prompts

Same prompt, same settings (1024×1024, default parameters). Different model.

01 Portrait
GPT-Image wins
Professional headshot of a 35-year-old woman with auburn hair, natural freckles, wearing a navy blazer, soft window lighting, shallow depth of field
Winner
GPT-Image 1.5 38.3s

Strong portrait with copper-red auburn hair, clearly visible freckles across the nose and cheeks, and a navy blazer. Soft window backlighting is evident and the background blur is subtle but appropriate. Age representation and framing are both convincing for a professional headshot.

Z-Image Turbo 1.3s

Solid professional portrait with auburn hair, light freckles, and a navy blazer against a soft window background. All criteria are technically met but the image has slightly less warmth and visual impact than the top scorers. The depth of field is less pronounced than ideal for the described shallow-DOF look.

02 Text Rendering
GPT-Image wins
Vintage coffee shop chalkboard menu with handwritten text 'DAILY SPECIALS' at the top, listing 'Espresso $3.50' and 'Cappuccino $4.75', rustic wooden frame
Winner
GPT-Image 1.5 38.1s

A visually rich chalkboard menu with perfectly accurate text, decorative chalk typography, elegant ornamental styling, and charming coffee cup illustrations. The warm wooden frame and classic chalk-art aesthetic create a strong vintage coffee shop feel. It ranks just behind the top entry due to slightly less environmental context but delivers exceptional prompt compliance and visual polish.

Z-Image Turbo 1.3s

All text is spelled and priced correctly, which is a clear win for legibility. The wooden frame is present and the chalkboard surface is rendered, but the lettering reads as printed block text rather than genuine handwritten chalk, losing the artisanal quality the prompt calls for. The overall aesthetic feels more modern and generic than authentically vintage.

03 Product
GPT-Image wins
Luxury perfume bottle on white marble surface, dramatic rim lighting, crystal-clear glass with amber liquid inside, minimalist product photography
Winner
GPT-Image 1.5 37.4s

Exceptional product photography with dramatic rim lighting that beautifully catches the bottle edges and illuminates the amber liquid. The dark background creates high contrast while the white marble adds luxury context. Surface reflections are clean and the overall image reads as top-tier commercial product photography.

Z-Image Turbo 1.9s

Clean professional product shot with good rim lighting creating edge definition on the rectangular bottle. The amber liquid is well-rendered and the composition is minimalist with a dark gradient background. Surface reflections exist but are understated compared to the best in the set.

04 Architecture
GPT-Image wins
Modern minimalist living room with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking city skyline at dusk, white sectional sofa, polished concrete floors
Winner
GPT-Image 1.5 40.4s

An outstanding execution with expansive floor-to-ceiling glazing framing a richly detailed night-transition cityscape, perfectly capturing the dusk-to-dark transition with city lights activating across the skyline. The off-white sectional, highly reflective polished concrete floor, and minimal furniture arrangement are all faithful to the brief. Interior ambient lighting is warm and photorealistic, with a convincing balance against the cooler exterior twilight.

Z-Image Turbo 1.6s

The scene correctly places a large white sectional in front of floor-to-ceiling windows with a city skyline at dusk, and polished concrete floors are clearly visible. Interior lighting appears flat and overly uniform, lacking the nuanced warm-cool interplay expected at dusk. The space reads slightly cramped and compressed, reducing the sense of architectural grandeur the prompt implies.

05 Fantasy
GPT-Image wins
Mystical elven warrior with silver armor and flowing white cape, holding a glowing blue sword, standing in ancient forest with magical particles
Winner
GPT-Image 1.5 45.3s

Well-executed fantasy illustration with a confident female elf warrior holding a prominently glowing blue sword in an ancient mossy forest. The flowing white cape is large and dynamic, and blue magical sparkles are scattered throughout the scene. The armor reads as dark steel rather than silver, falling short of the specified silver tone, but all other elements are faithfully present.

Z-Image Turbo 3.5s

Solid fantasy scene with a female elf warrior in full silver armor and a white cape, holding a bright blue glowing sword in a forested setting with rocky ground. Blue magical particle effects are visible. The image meets all criteria but has a somewhat generic presentation — the forest lacks the ancient, overgrown grandeur of the prompt and the magical ambiance feels understated.

06 Food
GPT-Image wins
Gourmet burger on wooden board, toasted brioche bun, melting cheddar cheese, crispy bacon, fresh lettuce, dripping sauce, food magazine style
Winner
GPT-Image 1.5 36.4s

An outstanding food magazine-quality image with every element of the prompt executed to a high standard. The brioche bun is perfectly golden and toasted, cheddar melts dramatically over thick crispy bacon, fresh lettuce peeks out attractively, and creamy sauce drips down onto the rustic wooden board. Shallow depth of field, warm studio lighting, and well-chosen props (glass, fries) elevate this to professional editorial level.

Z-Image Turbo 1.2s

A clean, technically correct burger image on a wooden cutting board with all key ingredients present: toasted bun, melting cheddar, bacon, lettuce, and dripping sauce. The composition feels modest and slightly flat compared to food-magazine standards—the angle is functional rather than aspirational, the background is understated, and there is little narrative or prop styling to elevate the image beyond a straightforward product shot.

07 Fashion
GPT-Image wins
Fashion model in oversized beige trench coat, standing on rainy Paris street at night, neon reflections on wet pavement, editorial Vogue style
Winner
GPT-Image 1.5 36.6s

A high-impact editorial image combining the Eiffel Tower, neon-lit Brasserie/Café signage, and brilliantly rendered warm-red reflections on the wet pavement. The model's frontal stance with hands in pockets is self-assured and distinctly Vogue in character, and the bold warm-cool neon contrast gives the image strong visual drama. The Vogue text overlay adds editorial context but is partially obscured, keeping the composition clean.

Z-Image Turbo 1.2s

Clean, well-composed fashion image with a classic oversized beige trench coat, wet Paris boulevard, and red-and-blue neon reflections on the pavement. The model's stance is editorial and confident. The color grading is relatively natural compared to some peers — technically sound but lacking the heightened drama and cinematic intensity that a top-tier Vogue editorial would demand.

08 Wildlife
GPT-Image wins
Majestic snow leopard resting on rocky mountain ledge, piercing blue-green eyes, thick spotted fur with snowflakes, golden hour backlighting
Winner
GPT-Image 1.5 39.5s

All eight criteria are satisfied in a single striking image: the leopard rests regally on a snow-dusted rocky ledge, blue-green eyes catching the warm golden light, with heavy and convincing snowfall throughout the scene. The golden-hour backlighting is dramatic and warm, and the spotted fur is sharply rendered. The close framing limits the full mountain panorama context slightly, but the composition is emotionally impactful and technically precise.

Z-Image Turbo 1.1s

The snow leopard itself is rendered with exceptional photographic realism — anatomy is accurate, the spotted fur is finely detailed, and the vivid blue-green eyes are striking. However the environmental context is nearly absent: the background is a plain warm blur with no discernible mountain ledge or snowy terrain, golden-hour backlighting is missing, and there are no snowflakes anywhere in the scene. The animal quality is high but the prompt's setting requirements are largely unmet.

09 Abstract
GPT-Image wins
Fluid abstract painting with swirling navy blue and gold metallic tones, organic flowing shapes, high contrast, modern art gallery piece
Winner
GPT-Image 1.5 34.8s

Strong fluid marbling with bold navy and glittering gold metallic veins and multiple visible swirl vortexes. However, prominent white and light-blue areas dilute the navy-and-gold palette away from the requested color scheme. The composition is dynamic but somewhat chaotic, and the image reads more as decorative stock art than a curated gallery piece.

Z-Image Turbo 1.1s

Deep navy with a strong centrifugal swirl radiating outward from a clear focal point, with golden veining providing high contrast. The composition is well-balanced with confident directional energy. The gold reads more as yellow-gold than metallic, lacking the reflective sheen implied by 'metallic tones,' and the image has no gallery context, giving it a decorative rather than fine-art feeling.

10 Sci-Fi
GPT-Image wins
Futuristic cyberpunk cityscape at night, massive holographic advertisements, flying vehicles between towering skyscrapers, rain-slicked streets with neon reflections
Winner
GPT-Image 1.5 53.4s

The most cinematically complete entry: massive holographic character displays (anime girl, armored robot), a floating MEGA CORP globe advertisement, multiple flying vehicles at varying distances, dense crowd with umbrellas, and vivid orange-purple neon reflecting off heavily rain-soaked streets. The layered depth from foreground street level to distant towers is exceptional, and the overall atmosphere feels genuinely futuristic.

Z-Image Turbo 1.2s

The image establishes a nighttime cyberpunk mood with a central tower, flanking skyscrapers, and flat billboard screens showing face portraits. Flying vehicles are present but sparse, and the billboard displays read as ordinary flat screens rather than holographic projections. Scene depth is limited by the portrait framing and compressed composition.
