FLUX.2 Turbo vs Seedream v4.5

We ran both models through 10 prompts and measured the results. Below you'll find the actual outputs, generation times, and our notes on each.

The 10 test prompts

Same prompt, same settings (1024×1024, default parameters). Different model.

01 Portrait
Seedream wins
Professional headshot of a 35-year-old woman with auburn hair, natural freckles, wearing a navy blazer, soft window lighting, shallow depth of field
FLUX.2 Turbo 3.1s

Competent headshot with auburn hair and navy blazer, clear window lighting from the side. Freckles are nearly invisible — only the faintest hint is present. The subject reads as late 30s to early 40s rather than 35, missing the specified age target noticeably.

Winner
Seedream v4.5 14.7s

Highest quality result: vibrant auburn hair, prominent natural freckles, navy blazer, and beautiful warm window light visible in the background. The skin texture is highly photorealistic and the subject looks naturally mid-30s. Freckles are more pronounced and natural-looking than any other image.

02 Text Rendering
Seedream wins
Vintage coffee shop chalkboard menu with handwritten text 'DAILY SPECIALS' at the top, listing 'Espresso $3.50' and 'Cappuccino $4.75', rustic wooden frame
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.1s

Text fidelity is excellent — all spellings and prices are accurate, delivered in a chalk-style font within a solid wooden frame. The chalkboard reads clearly and has a believable vintage tone. The design is functional but sparse in decorative detail, leaving it a step behind more expressive entries.

Winner
Seedream v4.5 31.7s

All specified text is correctly spelled and priced, rendered in a casual but convincing handwritten chalk style on an authentic-looking chalkboard with a real-wood frame. The photo-realistic rendering gives it a genuine, tactile quality. The composition is simpler and less ornate than the top performers, but it achieves a believable vintage coffee shop aesthetic with no errors.

03 Product
FLUX.2 Turbo wins
Luxury perfume bottle on white marble surface, dramatic rim lighting, crystal-clear glass with amber liquid inside, minimalist product photography
Winner
FLUX.2 Turbo 3.3s

Excellent product photography execution with a crystal-clear rectangular bottle under focused spot lighting on white marble. Rim lighting is evident in the halo effect on the marble below the bottle. All major prompt criteria are met with professional-quality execution.

Seedream v4.5 12.5s

Beautiful natural-light editorial image with a cylindrical bottle casting a dramatic warm shadow across white marble. The amber liquid and clear glass are well-executed, but the lighting style is editorial/natural rather than the controlled studio product photography feel specified. The marble and rim lighting are excellent.

04 Architecture
Seedream wins
Modern minimalist living room with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking city skyline at dusk, white sectional sofa, polished concrete floors
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.2s

A high-quality minimalist living room with corner floor-to-ceiling windows and a dusk skyline rendered with good tonal depth. The white sectional, polished concrete floor, and cove accent lighting all align well with the brief. The space feels open and architecturally plausible, though the skyline detail is slightly less intricate than the best entries.

Winner
Seedream v4.5 12.9s

The best-in-class result: a strikingly photorealistic rendering with floor-to-ceiling windows bathed in spectacular golden-hour dusk light, a highly reflective polished concrete floor, and a crisp white sectional in a beautifully spare minimalist space. The skyline is detailed and glowing with warm sunset tones reflecting off the city towers. Spatial depth and architectural scale are exceptional, making this feel like a real premium high-rise interior.

05 Fantasy
Seedream wins
Mystical elven warrior with silver armor and flowing white cape, holding a glowing blue sword, standing in ancient forest with magical particles
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.5s

Digital illustration style rendering of an elven warrior that checks all the key prompt elements: pointed ears, silver armor, flowing white cape, glowing blue sword, and a forest background with colorful magical particles. The flat illustration style and lower image resolution reduce visual impact compared to photorealistic entries, and the ancient forest setting lacks textural richness.

Winner
Seedream v4.5 16.8s

Outstanding cinematic fantasy image with a moonlit ancient forest setting and dramatic lighting. The elven warrior wears intricate white-silver armor and a large flowing white cape, holds a clearly glowing blue sword, and stands among twisted dark trees with scattered blue magical particles. All eight criteria are strongly met with exceptional compositional depth and atmosphere.

06 Food
Seedream wins
Gourmet burger on wooden board, toasted brioche bun, melting cheddar cheese, crispy bacon, fresh lettuce, dripping sauce, food magazine style
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.8s

A solid burger image with all required elements: sesame-topped toasted brioche bun, melting cheddar, crispy bacon, fresh lettuce, dripping sauce, and a wooden board. The composition is appealing but slightly soft and lacks the dramatic lighting or prop styling that food magazines typically demand. Textures are convincing and the overall feel is appetizing, placing it above average but not at editorial level.

Winner
Seedream v4.5 14.5s

Atmospheric and inviting food photography with warm amber lighting that suits the subject. The brioche bun is unfortunately over-charred with very dark burn marks, failing the toasted criterion convincingly. All other ingredients—melting cheddar, crispy bacon, fresh lettuce, and a dark BBQ-style dripping sauce—are well-represented on a proper wooden board. The overall styling reads as food-magazine quality despite the bun issue.

07 Fashion
Seedream wins
Fashion model in oversized beige trench coat, standing on rainy Paris street at night, neon reflections on wet pavement, editorial Vogue style
FLUX.2 Turbo 3.0s

Strong all-around execution with a clearly oversized beige trench coat, vivid neon reflections on a wet Paris boulevard, and a confident frontal editorial pose. The color grading delivers rich blue and red neon contrasts with a cinematic feel. A baked-in 'VOGUE' watermark gives it an editorial stamp but reads as slightly artificial rather than organically composed.

Winner
Seedream v4.5 13.5s

Dramatically cinematic with a striking blue-twilight sky, vibrant magenta/pink neon reflections on wet cobblestones, and the Eiffel Tower silhouetted in the background. The model's pose — three-quarter turn with face tilted upward — is powerfully editorial. The bold blue-to-magenta color palette is distinctive and atmospheric, elevating this beyond a standard fashion shot.

08 Wildlife
FLUX.2 Turbo wins
Majestic snow leopard resting on rocky mountain ledge, piercing blue-green eyes, thick spotted fur with snowflakes, golden hour backlighting
Winner
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.4s

The strongest result of the set: the snow leopard dominates the frame in a powerful frontal resting pose on a moss-and-snow-covered rocky ledge with mountains behind and vivid golden-hour rim light catching the fur. The blue-green eyes are brilliantly rendered and emotionally compelling, the large rosette spots on thick fur are exquisitely detailed, and golden bokeh-like snowflakes fall throughout the scene adding depth and atmosphere. Every criterion is met at a high level of technical and artistic quality.

Seedream v4.5 12.9s

A visually impressive image covering nearly all prompt elements: the leopard rests on a rocky ledge with snow-dusted mountains behind it, saturated blue-green eyes glow vividly, and falling snowflakes are visible on the fur. The warm golden-orange sky and mountain silhouettes reinforce the golden hour atmosphere well. The rendering style is somewhat painterly and the eye color is hyper-saturated beyond natural range, slightly undermining photorealism.

09 Abstract
Seedream wins
Fluid abstract painting with swirling navy blue and gold metallic tones, organic flowing shapes, high contrast, modern art gallery piece
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.2s

Framed canvas in a minimal gallery setting with deep navy blues interweaving with gold metallic streams and visible paint texture variation. Swirling motion flows naturally across the canvas with good organic variety. Some lighter silverish-blue areas add tonal richness without undermining the navy palette. Gallery presentation and composition balance are both strong.

Winner
Seedream v4.5 16.9s

Exceptional gallery-context presentation with the painting shown hung on a white wall under track lighting. Deep saturated navy blues are dominated by a luminous gold metallic river flowing diagonally across the canvas with iridescent highlights adding visual depth. All prompt criteria are met at a high level — swirling motion is dynamic, contrast is striking, and the fluid paint texture reads as genuinely painterly.

10 Sci-Fi
Seedream wins
Futuristic cyberpunk cityscape at night, massive holographic advertisements, flying vehicles between towering skyscrapers, rain-slicked streets with neon reflections
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.2s

A portrait-mode cyberpunk street scene with a robotic-figure billboard at center, pink and purple neon atmosphere, flying ships overhead, and flanking skyscrapers. Rain and wet-street reflections are present but understated, and the square portrait crop limits the sense of cityscape breadth. Visually competent but lacks the density and cinematic scale of the top performers.

Winner
Seedream v4.5 12.8s

A dramatic low-angle road composition with massive NEO TECH and CYBER FASHION text blazing across building faces and a luminous cylindrical holographic structure at center. Rain-slicked asphalt with vivid cyan and magenta reflections is the visual highlight, executed with exceptional quality. Flying vehicles are nearly absent, appearing only as tiny barely-visible shapes in the far background.
