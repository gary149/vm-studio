The 10 test prompts

Same prompt, same settings (1024×1024, default parameters). Different model.

01 Portrait
FLUX.2 Turbo wins
Professional headshot of a 35-year-old woman with auburn hair, natural freckles, wearing a navy blazer, soft window lighting, shallow depth of field
Winner
FLUX.2 Turbo 3.1s

Highly realistic portrait with excellent freckle rendering that appears very natural. The auburn hair color is vibrant and accurate, and the navy blazer is well-rendered. Window lighting from the left creates pleasing soft shadows. However, the subject appears slightly older than 35, closer to early 40s, which affects age accuracy.

Z-Image Turbo 1.3s

Clean professional portrait with good window lighting and proper depth of field. The auburn hair and navy blazer are accurately rendered. However, the skin is noticeably too smooth and plastic-looking, lacking realistic texture and pores. Freckles are extremely sparse and barely visible. The subject appears to be in her late 30s to early 40s, slightly older than the target age.

02 Text Rendering
Z-Image wins
Vintage coffee shop chalkboard menu with handwritten text 'DAILY SPECIALS' at the top, listing 'Espresso $3.50' and 'Cappuccino $4.75', rustic wooden frame
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.1s

Text is correctly rendered: 'DAILY SPECIALS', 'Espresso $3.50', and 'Cappuccino $4.75' all spelled accurately. Good chalkboard aesthetic with decorative underline and a rustic wooden frame. Slightly less polished presentation than top-tier models but solid text accuracy.

Winner
Z-Image Turbo 1.3s

Excellent text accuracy with 'DAILY SPECIALS', 'Espresso $3.50', and 'Cappuccino $4.75' all correctly rendered. Clean, minimalist chalkboard design with good wooden frame. Slightly simpler aesthetic compared to top performers but flawless text execution.

03 Product
FLUX.2 Turbo wins
Luxury perfume bottle on white marble surface, dramatic rim lighting, crystal-clear glass with amber liquid inside, minimalist product photography
Winner
FLUX.2 Turbo 3.3s

Clean, studio-quality product shot with excellent glass transparency and accurate amber liquid coloring. The spotlight effect on the marble creates an elegant circular highlight that draws focus to the product. The marble veining is subtle but realistic. While the image lacks strong rim lighting (the edge glow is minimal), the overall commercial quality remains high with good minimalist composition.

Z-Image Turbo 1.9s

Decent product shot with acceptable glass transparency and good amber liquid color. The marble surface shows realistic texture. However, the rim lighting effect is very weak, and the overall image has a slightly soft, less polished quality compared to the others. The bottle shape appears slightly less refined, and the spray mechanism looks somewhat generic. Would need post-processing to reach true commercial standards.

04 Architecture
FLUX.2 Turbo wins
Modern minimalist living room with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking city skyline at dusk, white sectional sofa, polished concrete floors
Winner
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.2s

Strong floor-to-ceiling windows with an impressive city skyline, but the lighting reads more as twilight/early evening rather than characteristic golden-hour dusk tones. The polished concrete floors have excellent reflective quality, and the white sectional is well-executed. Overall very realistic but missing the warm dusk color palette.

Z-Image Turbo 1.6s

Solid minimalist composition with clean white sectional and good polished concrete floors. The floor-to-ceiling windows show a city skyline, but the lighting appears more neutral daylight or overcast rather than dusk. The windows also do not wrap around corners, making the space feel less expansive. The image is realistic but lacks the dramatic atmosphere of the prompt.

05 Fantasy
FLUX.2 Turbo wins
Mystical elven warrior with silver armor and flowing white cape, holding a glowing blue sword, standing in ancient forest with magical particles
Winner
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.5s

Well-executed fantasy warrior with very prominent pointed ears and ethereal appearance. The silver-toned armor has a unique spiked shoulder design, and the white cape flows naturally. The bright glowing blue sword is excellently rendered, and the mystical forest with floating particles creates strong atmosphere. Full-body composition is well balanced.

Z-Image Turbo 3.5s

Competent rendering of the elven warrior with visible pointed ears and silver plate armor. The white cape is present and the glowing blue sword effect is decent. However, the magical particle effects are minimal compared to other models, and the overall composition feels more standard and less cinematic. The forest environment is adequate but lacks the mystical depth of higher-scoring images.

06 Food
Z-Image wins
Gourmet burger on wooden board, toasted brioche bun, melting cheddar cheese, crispy bacon, fresh lettuce, dripping sauce, food magazine style
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.8s

Solid execution with a nice golden brioche bun and good cheese drip effect. The lettuce is fresh and vibrant, and bacon texture is visible. The composition is somewhat plain compared to others, missing the extra styling elements that make it magazine-worthy.

Winner
Z-Image Turbo 1.2s

Clean, modern food magazine composition with nice props including a magazine in the background. The bacon is perfectly crispy and sauce drips nicely. However, the bun lacks the golden toasted appearance of brioche, and the cheese could melt more dramatically.

07 Fashion
FLUX.2 Turbo wins
Fashion model in oversized beige trench coat, standing on rainy Paris street at night, neon reflections on wet pavement, editorial Vogue style
Winner
FLUX.2 Turbo 3.0s

Strong editorial execution with a perfectly centered model in a well-fitted beige trench coat. Haussmannian architecture and wet Parisian streets are clearly rendered with colorful neon reflections. The confident centered pose nails the editorial brief, though the composition feels slightly more commercial than high-fashion artistic.

Z-Image Turbo 1.2s

Excellent street photography aesthetic with a truly oversized trench coat and confident walking pose. The wet pavement reflections with red neon create good atmosphere. While the Parisian setting is implied through architecture rather than landmarks, the overall photorealistic quality and natural model styling feel authentically editorial.

08 Wildlife
FLUX.2 Turbo wins
Majestic snow leopard resting on rocky mountain ledge, piercing blue-green eyes, thick spotted fur with snowflakes, golden hour backlighting
Winner
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.4s

Strong photorealistic rendering with beautiful golden backlighting and visible snowfall in the scene. The snow leopard's markings and thick fur are accurately depicted in a confident forward-facing pose. However, the eyes appear more amber/neutral rather than the specified blue-green color.

Z-Image Turbo 1.1s

Clean, photorealistic portrait of a snow leopard with striking blue eyes and accurate spotted coat markings. The pose is dignified and the rock ledge is present. However, the background is blurred warm tones rather than a mountain scene, no snowflakes are visible, and the golden hour effect is subtle to absent.

09 Abstract
FLUX.2 Turbo wins
Fluid abstract painting with swirling navy blue and gold metallic tones, organic flowing shapes, high contrast, modern art gallery piece
Winner
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.2s

Strong abstract composition with dynamic swirling patterns and cosmic energy. Good balance of navy blue and gold tones with interesting splatter details. The framed gallery presentation works well. Movement feels slightly more chaotic than elegant, and the smaller image size limits detail appreciation.

Z-Image Turbo 1.1s

Achieves the basic fluid abstract style with navy blue and gold tones in organic flowing shapes. However, the contrast is less dramatic, and the overall execution feels more generic. Small image size and lack of gallery context diminishes the premium feel. Competent but lacks the sophistication of higher-ranked entries.

10 Sci-Fi
Z-Image wins
Futuristic cyberpunk cityscape at night, massive holographic advertisements, flying vehicles between towering skyscrapers, rain-slicked streets with neon reflections
FLUX.2 Turbo 2.2s

Good rendition with prominent holographic whale/creature displays and flying vehicles between buildings. Rain and wet street reflections are visible, though the overall palette feels more desaturated gray-blue than the vibrant neon typically associated with cyberpunk. The scene feels slightly sterile compared to more atmospheric competitors.

Winner
Z-Image Turbo 1.2s

Clean, polished composition with impressive towering central skyscraper, curved billboard displays showing faces, and flying vehicles in the mid-ground. The wet street reflections and muted teal-pink color palette create an authentic cyberpunk atmosphere with good depth and scale, though slightly less visually dense than top performers.
