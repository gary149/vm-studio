01 Portrait
FLUX.2 Klein wins
Professional headshot of a 35-year-old woman with auburn hair, natural freckles, wearing a navy blazer, soft window lighting, shallow depth of field
Winner
FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.9s

Exceptional photorealistic quality with natural skin texture showing pores and subtle imperfections. Freckles are clearly visible and naturally distributed across the face. The navy blazer with white blouse is perfectly rendered, soft window lighting creates beautiful catchlights in the eyes, and the shallow depth of field with blurred background is professionally executed.

Seedream v4.5 14.7s

Polished portrait with good professional composition and warm lighting. The auburn hair is rendered with attractive volume and waves. However, the skin is overly smoothed with an airbrushed quality lacking natural texture. Freckles are minimal and barely noticeable. The subject appears to be in her late 20s rather than 35, giving an overly glamorized look.

03 Product
FLUX.2 Klein wins
Luxury perfume bottle on white marble surface, dramatic rim lighting, crystal-clear glass with amber liquid inside, minimalist product photography
Winner
FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.5s

Beautiful product photography with strong directional lighting that creates dramatic shadows and highlights. The glass bottle shows excellent transparency with visible light refraction. The amber liquid has rich, warm tones. The marble surface features realistic veining and good reflectivity. The composition is clean and professional with elegant proportions suitable for commercial use.

Seedream v4.5 12.5s

The weakest of the six images. While the amber liquid color is pleasant and the marble has visible veining, the bottle design is a simple cylinder lacking the crystal-clear faceted glass requested. The image has a more casual, lifestyle aesthetic rather than dramatic rim-lit product photography. The glass appears more like regular clear glass without the premium refraction qualities. Background elements and softer lighting reduce the minimalist, commercial impact.

Winner
Seedream v4.5 12.9s

Outstanding polished concrete floor with excellent sheen and reflective properties. The dusk lighting captures golden hour beautifully with buildings illuminated against the sunset gradient. Winner
Seedream v4.5 16.8s

Exceptional execution across all criteria. The elven warrior has distinctly pointed ears and ethereal features, with intricately detailed silver armor featuring ornate engravings.
