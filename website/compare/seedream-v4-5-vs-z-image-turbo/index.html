The 10 test prompts

Same prompt, same settings (1024×1024, default parameters). Different model.

01 Portrait
Seedream wins
Professional headshot of a 35-year-old woman with auburn hair, natural freckles, wearing a navy blazer, soft window lighting, shallow depth of field
Winner
Seedream v4.5 14.7s

The strongest image in the set: warm golden window light streams in from the upper right, producing soft catchlights and a distinctly editorial glow that matches the 'soft window lighting' prompt precisely. Freckles are abundant and naturally distributed across the nose and cheeks, skin texture is rendered with fine-pore realism, and the navy blazer sits cleanly over a white blouse. The composition is tight and centered with a pleasing bokeh falloff behind the subject.

Z-Image Turbo 1.3s

Sharp and technically clean with vivid auburn hair, a confident direct gaze, and well-rendered freckles scattered across the cheeks and nose. Soft window glow is visible in the blurred background, and the navy blazer is accurately rendered. The skin texture is detailed and photorealistic; the image reads as slightly cooler in tone compared to the top two, which slightly reduces the warmth implied by 'soft window lighting.'

02 Text Rendering
Seedream wins
Vintage coffee shop chalkboard menu with handwritten text 'DAILY SPECIALS' at the top, listing 'Espresso $3.50' and 'Cappuccino $4.75', rustic wooden frame
Winner
Seedream v4.5 31.7s

Clean and natural handwritten chalk text with a horizontal rule under the header; wooden frame edges with visible wood grain are authentic. Warm ambient side-lighting adds realism. All spelling and prices are correct. The composition is straightforward but the genuine chalk texture and realistic framing make it stand out as believable signage.

Z-Image Turbo 1.3s

All required text and prices are present and correctly spelled. The board is mounted on a dark wooden plank wall with a matching wooden frame. However the lettering reads as a clean printed or stenciled font rather than genuine chalk handwriting, which undermines the core brief. Minimal decoration and no café environment context limit the atmosphere.

03 Product
Seedream wins
Luxury perfume bottle on white marble surface, dramatic rim lighting, crystal-clear glass with amber liquid inside, minimalist product photography
Winner
Seedream v4.5 12.5s

Cylindrical bottle with rose-gold cap sits on richly veined white marble, bathed in warm natural side light that casts a glowing amber shadow across the surface — the most evocative and editorial composition in the set. All criteria are fully met: the glass is crystal-clear, the honey-amber liquid radiates warmth, and the marble reflections are stunning. The naturalistic golden-hour mood elevates this beyond standard product photography into genuine visual artistry.

Z-Image Turbo 1.9s

Rectangular bottle with a clear/glass cap and amber-golden liquid on a clean white marble surface with subtle veining; a strong right-edge highlight delivers clear rim lighting. The composition is very minimal and clean, but the marble surface reflections are nearly absent, and the overall image has less depth and visual tension than the higher-ranked entries. Technically correct but lacks the finishing details that make luxury product shots memorable.

04 Architecture
Seedream wins
Modern minimalist living room with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking city skyline at dusk, white sectional sofa, polished concrete floors
Winner
Seedream v4.5 12.9s

Wide-angle view of a near-empty minimalist loft with large floor-to-ceiling glass panels and a dramatically lit golden-hour skyline — tall downtown towers catching the last warm sunlight against a blue-gradient sky. The white sectional sits on highly polished reflective concrete, and the sparse staging emphasizes architectural grandeur. Slightly barren decor keeps it from the very top tier but the dusk drama and floor quality are excellent.

Z-Image Turbo 1.6s

Corner floor-to-ceiling glazing and a white L-shaped sectional are correctly rendered on smooth concrete floors. However, the sky outside reads as pale daytime-to-early-evening rather than the dramatic dusk the prompt specifies, and interior lighting lacks the warm ambient glow expected at that hour. The composition is straightforward but lacks the spatial drama, depth, and polish of stronger entries.

05 Fantasy
Seedream wins
Mystical elven warrior with silver armor and flowing white cape, holding a glowing blue sword, standing in ancient forest with magical particles
Winner
Seedream v4.5 16.8s

Cinematic moonlit composition with a regal elven warrior (tiara headpiece, pointed ears, long white hair) in intricate white-silver plate armor and a long flowing white cape. The blue glowing sword is held lowered at the side, and blue magical particles dot the gnarled moonlit forest. The nocturnal atmosphere is the most immersive of the set; the sword pose is passive rather than aggressive, which slightly reduces impact.

Z-Image Turbo 3.5s

Smaller-format digital illustration of a female elf warrior in silver armor with flowing white cape and blue glowing sword standing in a dense forest with scattered blue particles. All required prompt elements are present and the composition is legible, but the rendering lacks fine detail — armor surfaces are low-texture, facial refinement is minimal, and the particle effects are barely visible, resulting in the weakest overall technical execution of the eight images.

06 Food
Seedream wins
Gourmet burger on wooden board, toasted brioche bun, melting cheddar cheese, crispy bacon, fresh lettuce, dripping sauce, food magazine style
Winner
Seedream v4.5 14.5s

Warm amber lighting creates an appealing atmosphere and the melting cheddar is well-rendered; crispy bacon and fresh lettuce are clearly visible, and a dark dripping sauce (reads BBQ) flows down the side of the patty onto the board. The main drawback is the bun, which is over-charred on top — the blackened char detracts from the polish expected of a gourmet food magazine shot.

Z-Image Turbo 1.2s

Clean, bright composition on a wooden board with a newspaper prop suggesting editorial context; melting cheddar drips neatly and bacon and lettuce are present. However, the bun reads as a plain round roll rather than a distinctively brioche bun, and the overall lighting is flat and more catalog-like than editorial food magazine. Texture realism is adequate but less nuanced than the top-ranked images.

07 Fashion
Seedream wins
Fashion model in oversized beige trench coat, standing on rainy Paris street at night, neon reflections on wet pavement, editorial Vogue style
Winner
Seedream v4.5 13.5s

Dynamic and cinematic: the model grips her oversized beige trench lapel in a three-quarter over-the-shoulder pose, with visible rain streaks against the Eiffel Tower looming behind her. The cobblestone pavement glows with vivid magenta and blue neon reflections creating a moody, saturated palette. The pose and atmospheric detail are strong, but the composition leans more toward commercial photography than strict editorial Vogue framing, lacking the typographic or graphic polish of higher-ranked images.

Z-Image Turbo 1.2s

A competent execution of the core brief — beige double-breasted trench, wet street, night setting, hands-in-pockets stance — but the image feels like a product shot rather than an editorial spread. The neon reflections on the pavement are present but muted, the background depth is shallow, and the overall color grading is flat without distinctive atmosphere or cinematic punch. The model's pose is confident but generic, and no Paris-specific visual cues beyond the street composition are visible.

08 Wildlife
Seedream wins
Majestic snow leopard resting on rocky mountain ledge, piercing blue-green eyes, thick spotted fur with snowflakes, golden hour backlighting
Winner
Seedream v4.5 12.9s

All prompt elements are present — vivid teal eyes, snow-covered rocky ledge, warm golden hour sky, and correct spotted patterning — but the rendering leans slightly illustrative rather than photorealistic, with oversaturated orange sky and hyper-vivid eye colors that push the image toward concept art rather than wildlife photography. The upright alert pose reads as majestic and the snow coverage is excellent.

Z-Image Turbo 1.1s

Tight crop of the snow leopard resting on a rocky surface with clear blue-green eyes and accurate spotted fur; snowflakes are lightly dusted on the coat. The anatomy and photorealism are good, but the extremely shallow depth of field eliminates any mountain ledge context and the background is a plain warm bokeh rather than a golden hour scene. The missing environmental framing significantly reduces prompt adherence.

09 Abstract
Seedream wins
Fluid abstract painting with swirling navy blue and gold metallic tones, organic flowing shapes, high contrast, modern art gallery piece
Winner
Seedream v4.5 16.9s

Presented as an actual gallery-hung painting with track lighting and white walls, seedream-v4-5 delivers the most complete interpretation: deep navy blue fields contrast dramatically against luminous, broad-stroke gold forms with visible painterly brushwork. The diagonal swirl from lower-left to upper-right gives the composition tremendous energy, and the near-black shadow zones push contrast to the maximum. Minor iridescent prismatic glints in the gold add interest without feeling accidental.

Z-Image Turbo 1.1s

z-image-turbo produces a clean, centered spiral composition with rich navy and warm gold in a marble-fluid style. The radial symmetry from the center creates a strong focal anchor, and the gold has a genuinely metallic quality. The piece lacks a gallery presentation context and the composition's balance, while pleasing, feels somewhat expected and safe; the fluid texture is smooth but lacks painterly depth compared to higher-ranked entries.

10 Sci-Fi
Seedream wins
Futuristic cyberpunk cityscape at night, massive holographic advertisements, flying vehicles between towering skyscrapers, rain-slicked streets with neon reflections
Winner
Seedream v4.5 12.8s

Technically the most polished street-level render in terms of wet road surface quality — the teal and magenta neon reflections on the rain-slicked road are superb, and 'NEO TECH' and 'CYBER FASHION' signage reads clearly on flanking skyscrapers. However, flying vehicles are essentially absent (only a faint drone silhouette in the far background fails the criterion), which is a meaningful miss for this prompt. The narrower field of view and ground-only vehicle presence limit the futuristic cityscape feel.

Z-Image Turbo 1.2s

The scene covers the core cyberpunk elements — billboard ads, flying vehicles, skyscrapers, wet streets with neon reflections — but execution is noticeably weaker than the rest of the field. The image appears lower resolution with softer detail rendering, the atmospheric depth is limited, and the billboard faces feel generic. The color palette is competent but lacks the richness and haze layering that elevates the stronger entries.
