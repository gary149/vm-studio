The 10 test prompts

01 Portrait
FLUX.2 Klein wins
Professional headshot of a 35-year-old woman with auburn hair, natural freckles, wearing a navy blazer, soft window lighting, shallow depth of field
Nano Banana Pro 18.6s

A warm, candid-feeling portrait with clear window light streaming from the left and a pleasantly blurred background showing plants and shelving. Auburn hair, freckles, and a navy blazer are all present. However, the skin rendering is notably smoother and less textured than the top tier images — pore detail and micro-texture are absent, giving a slightly painterly feel. The composition has good natural warmth but lacks the technical sharpness expected from a professional headshot.

Winner
FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.9s

A highly polished professional headshot with vivid auburn hair, a well-fitted navy blazer, and clearly visible freckles across the nose bridge. The background shows a blurred window and greenery, providing strong environmental support for the window-lighting requirement. Skin rendering is smooth yet realistic, and the three-quarter gaze into camera gives the image a confident, editorial presence.

02 Text Rendering
Nano Banana wins
Vintage coffee shop chalkboard menu with handwritten text 'DAILY SPECIALS' at the top, listing 'Espresso $3.50' and 'Cappuccino $4.75', rustic wooden frame
Winner
Nano Banana Pro 21.4s

The most photorealistic and immersive entry: a weathered chalkboard on an easel inside a brick-walled café, with a beautifully aged wooden frame showing peeling paint. All required text is correctly spelled and both prices appear cleanly. Handwritten script and chalk coffee-cup illustrations add warmth without cluttering the required content.

FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.5s

All spelling and prices are correct, and the chalk lettering is readable and reasonably handwritten. The wooden frame is present and the warm, slightly faded background conveys a vintage café atmosphere. The image lacks decorative flourishes or scene context, making it functional but less visually rich than higher-ranked entries.

03 Product
Nano Banana wins
Luxury perfume bottle on white marble surface, dramatic rim lighting, crystal-clear glass with amber liquid inside, minimalist product photography
Winner
Nano Banana Pro 16.7s

Tall faceted hexagonal bottle labeled 'ÉCLAT D'OR' on a white marble slab, with a dark background that amplifies the dramatic rim lighting — the angular facets catch the backlight beautifully and create crisp edge highlights. Deep amber-gold liquid fills the body with satisfying richness, and the overall cinematic tension between dark surround and glowing glass is excellent. The top of the bottle is slightly cropped but the overall impact is highly polished and luxury-brand worthy.

FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.5s

Clean rectangular flacon with a crystal-and-gold spray cap stands on white marble with visible gray veining, lit by crisp side lighting that creates well-defined rim highlights on both vertical edges. The amber liquid is rich and the marble reflections are clean. The composition is slightly conventional compared to the top-ranked entries, but technical execution is solid and would pass commercial review.

04 Architecture
FLUX.2 Klein wins
Modern minimalist living room with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking city skyline at dusk, white sectional sofa, polished concrete floors
Nano Banana Pro 19.0s

Large floor-to-ceiling windows wrap two walls with a vivid twilight skyline at dusk. The white sectional is well-proportioned and concrete floors are visible beneath an area rug. Wooden bookshelf ends and a chunky wooden coffee table introduce a warmer, less minimalist aesthetic that slightly pulls from the clean contemporary brief, though the dusk lighting and spatial depth remain strong.

Winner
FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.5s

Generous U-shaped white sectional fills a clean minimalist room with corner floor-to-ceiling windows showing a pink-orange dusk skyline. The space is very tidy and compositionally balanced, with visible polished concrete and only a small round coffee table as accent. The image reads slightly flat in terms of depth cues and artistic staging compared to higher-ranked entries.

05 Fantasy
Nano Banana wins
Mystical elven warrior with silver armor and flowing white cape, holding a glowing blue sword, standing in ancient forest with magical particles
Winner
Nano Banana Pro 18.5s

Cinematic three-quarter profile pose of a female elf (braided white hair, pointed ears) in engraved silver armor with a flowing tattered white cape set against moss-covered ancient trunks. Teal and blue magical particles drift throughout the forest depth, and the blue sword emits a clear luminescent glow. The unconventional pose and atmospheric depth are strengths; the relatively dim sword glow and worn cape edges read as stylistic choices rather than errors.

FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.5s

Illustration-style render in a smaller square format showing a female elf (pointed ears, white hair) in silver plate armor with a white cape and blue glowing sword held forward. The ancient forest background includes faint blue sparkles. All core prompt elements are present and coherent, but the illustration style is noticeably flatter and less detailed than peer entries — armor texturing lacks depth and the particle effects are sparse.

06 Food
Nano Banana wins
Gourmet burger on wooden board, toasted brioche bun, melting cheddar cheese, crispy bacon, fresh lettuce, dripping sauce, food magazine style
Winner
Nano Banana Pro 15.8s

Clean white-studio editorial style with excellent natural light; the brioche bun is golden with a soft rounded crown and slight sheen, and melting cheddar drips attractively down the side. Crispy bacon and fresh lettuce are well-represented, and the wooden board with garnish (pickle spear, rosemary) adds polish. Slightly less dramatic than the top entries but confidently magazine-ready.

FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.4s

Strong food photography execution with warm editorial lighting and a beautiful cheddar melt cascading across the bun and patty; crispy bacon strips and fresh lettuce are clearly present, and the wooden board is well-integrated. A decorative magazine-cover text overlay (non-obstructive) frames the image, reinforcing the editorial intent. Loses a few points relative to the top tier for the overlay and slightly less compositional depth.

07 Fashion
FLUX.2 Klein wins
Fashion model in oversized beige trench coat, standing on rainy Paris street at night, neon reflections on wet pavement, editorial Vogue style
Nano Banana Pro 19.4s

The Paris atmosphere is strong — Eiffel Tower visible through rain, cobblestones, vintage Parisian street scene — and the beige trench coat is accurate in color. However, the model's pose is undermined by holding an umbrella that partially obscures her face and creates a hunched, defensive posture that reads as street-snapshot rather than editorial. The wet cobblestone scene lacks distinct neon color reflections, and the overall mood is documentary rather than fashion-forward.

Winner
FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.3s

A clean, confident editorial frame: the model stands centered in a Parisian canyon street, wearing a long beige double-breasted trench over a black turtleneck, her upright posture and direct gaze projecting high-fashion authority. Multi-colored neon signs (including a prominent VARLE sign) cast rainbow reflections across rain-drenched cobblestones, with shop fronts and parked cars providing convincing urban depth. The color grading is cool and controlled, though slightly restrained in atmosphere compared to the top two entries.

08 Wildlife
FLUX.2 Klein wins
Majestic snow leopard resting on rocky mountain ledge, piercing blue-green eyes, thick spotted fur with snowflakes, golden hour backlighting
Nano Banana Pro 18.5s

The snow leopard is positioned on a snowy rocky ledge with warm golden side-light and teal eyes visible, but the crouching, slightly hunched posture reads as cautious or alert rather than majestic and resting. The rendering quality is decent but the smaller animal-to-frame ratio and less dramatic lighting reduce overall impact. Snowflake detail is minimal compared to competitors.

Winner
FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.5s

Solid prompt adherence with the leopard resting on a snow-dusted ledge and warm golden side-light from behind illuminating the mountain backdrop. Eyes have a cool blue-teal tone and spotting is anatomically plausible. The overall image is competent but lacks the dramatic lighting intensity or compositional ambition seen in the top-ranked images, resulting in a pleasant but less memorable execution.

09 Abstract
FLUX.2 Klein wins
Fluid abstract painting with swirling navy blue and gold metallic tones, organic flowing shapes, high contrast, modern art gallery piece
Nano Banana Pro 19.3s

nano-banana-pro is presented in a wide gallery room which lends strong environmental context, and the diagonal flow and organic shapes are well-executed. The critical weakness is color: the dominant blue reads as steel-grey or slate rather than navy, significantly missing the prompt's color specification, and this desaturation also reduces apparent contrast. The gold streaks are bold and warm, but they fight against a grey-blue ground rather than the expected deep navy.

Winner
FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.4s

flux2-klein-9b delivers a competent marble-pattern image with smooth navy-and-gold bands in a classic S-curve arrangement. The execution is clean and the gold is warm and legible, but the composition is predictable and the contrast between blue and gold is moderate rather than high. The piece lacks the drama or visual tension needed for gallery caliber — it reads more as polished decorative art than a statement fine-art work.

10 Sci-Fi
Nano Banana wins
Futuristic cyberpunk cityscape at night, massive holographic advertisements, flying vehicles between towering skyscrapers, rain-slicked streets with neon reflections
Winner
Nano Banana Pro 19.1s

A beautifully rendered Neo-Tokyo boulevard with 'NEO-TOKYO' and 'CYBER-RAMEN' large-format billboard ads, umbrella-carrying pedestrians in falling rain, and at least three clearly visible flying vehicles at mid-height. The deep-blue palette with warm accent lighting is polished and cohesive, and the rain-slicked pavement carries strong neon reflections. Slightly less raw visual density than the top tier, but composition, scale, and technical execution are excellent.

FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.4s

Wide-angle street corridor with large portrait-face billboard ads in blue-purple tones, several flying craft visible above the road, and wet pavement with neon reflections. The composition is clean and covers all prompt elements, but the execution feels somewhat flatter and less atmospheric than flux2-turbo — less rain haze, less surface grime, and the billboard ads feel more like standard screens than immersive holographic displays. Good baseline execution without outstanding artistic distinction.
