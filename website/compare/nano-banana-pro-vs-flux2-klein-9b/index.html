Nano Banana Pro 18.6s

Natural and warm portrait with excellent professional composition and authentic window lighting. The auburn hair is beautifully rendered and the navy blazer with cream blouse looks professional. Winner
FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.9s

Exceptional photorealistic quality with natural skin texture showing pores and subtle imperfections. Freckles are clearly visible and naturally distributed across the face. The navy blazer with white blouse is perfectly rendered, soft window lighting creates beautiful catchlights in the eyes, and the shallow depth of field with blurred background is professionally executed.

05 Fantasy

Winner
FLUX.2 Klein 9B 1.5s

Good elven warrior depiction with clearly visible pointed ears and white hair. The silver armor is cleanly rendered and the white cape flows behind the figure. The glowing blue sword is well done with good luminosity. The twisted tree forest creates atmosphere, but the overall composition feels slightly more illustrative than epic, with a more static pose.

06 Food
Nano Banana wins
Gourmet burger on wooden board, toasted brioche bun, melting cheddar cheese, crispy bacon, fresh lettuce, dripping sauce, food magazine style
Winner
Nano Banana Pro 15.8s

Exceptional food photography with perfect magazine styling. The beautifully toasted golden brioche bun, dramatic dripping sauce, crispy bacon, and melting cheddar are all impeccably rendered. 07 Fashion

Winner

Good execution of all core elements with rainbow neon reflections on wet pavement and clear night setting. The trench coat and model pose are well-rendered. However, the composition feels slightly more documentary than high-fashion editorial, and the overall lighting lacks the dramatic, polished quality of a Vogue shoot.
