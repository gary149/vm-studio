## FAQ Section

### How accurate is the text rendering?
For typical use cases—headlines, short phrases, names, labels—GPT-Image 1.5 achieves near-perfect accuracy. Longer text blocks may occasionally have minor errors, though accuracy remains significantly higher than any other image generation model.

### Can I use GPT-Image 1.5 for commercial projects?
Yes. Images generated through both OpenAI's API and fal.ai are cleared for commercial use according to each provider's terms.

### What's the difference between OpenAI direct vs. fal.ai?
The generated images are identical. The differences are in pricing, billing, and account management.

### Does GPT-Image 1.5 support image-to-image generation?
Yes. You can provide a reference image and prompt GPT-Image 1.5 to modify it while maintaining text accuracy.
