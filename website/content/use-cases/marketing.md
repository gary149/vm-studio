## FAQ Section

### How fast can I generate campaign assets?
Most images generate in 5-15 seconds. With batch generation, you can create 8 variations in under 2 minutes.

### Can I maintain brand consistency across generated images?
Yes. Include your brand guidelines in prompts (colors, style references, mood) and they carry through every generation. Save your best prompts and reuse them.

### What image sizes work for social media and ads?
VM Studio supports 10 aspect ratios including 1:1 (Instagram), 16:9 (LinkedIn, YouTube), 9:16 (Stories, Reels), and 4:5 (Instagram feed). Resolution goes up to 4K.

### How does this compare to stock photography?
Stock photos are generic and overused. AI-generated images are unique to your brand, cost cents per image, and have no licensing headaches.
