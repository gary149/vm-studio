## FAQ Section

**Section Title:** Text-to-Image Questions

### How do I write better prompts?
Be specific about what you want: subject, style, lighting, angle, and mood. Start with the main subject, then add modifiers. "A red chair" works, but "A mid-century modern red leather armchair, studio lighting, white cyclorama background, product photography" works better.

### Which model should I use for text-to-image?
For most cases, start with FLUX.2 Turbo or Nano Banana Pro. If you need text in your image (logos, signs, UI), use GPT-Image 1.5. For cinematic quality at 4K, use Seedream v4.5. For rapid iteration, Z-Image Turbo is fastest.

### Can I generate multiple images at once?
Yes. Use the batch slider to generate up to 4 images per prompt. They appear in a grid on your canvas, automatically positioned to avoid existing elements.

### What resolutions are available?
Depending on the model, you can output at 1K (1024px), 2K (2048px), or 4K (4096px) on the longest edge. FLUX.2 models and GPT-Image 1.5 support 1K. Nano Banana Pro, Seedream, and Z-Image support up to 4K.

### How much does text-to-image generation cost?
VM Studio is free. You pay only for API usage directly to your chosen provider. Costs vary by model and resolution. A typical 1K image costs $0.01-0.05 depending on the model. Check fal.ai, OpenAI, or OpenRouter pricing for exact rates.
