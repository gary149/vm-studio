## How BYOK Works

### Your keys. Your costs. Your control.

**1. Get Your API Key**
Sign up with any supported provider (fal.ai, OpenAI, Google AI Studio, or OpenRouter) and generate an API key. Most offer free credits to start.

**2. Add It to VM Studio**
Paste your API key into VM Studio's settings. Keys are stored locally on your device and never touch our servers.

**3. Generate and Pay Direct**
When you generate images, requests go directly to the provider. You pay them at their published rates. We add zero markup.

## FAQ Section

### Is VM Studio really free?
Yes. The Figma plugin costs nothing. You only pay the AI providers for the images you generate.

### Which provider should I start with?
**Google AI Studio** offers up to 500 free images per day. **fal.ai** offers the widest model selection. **OpenAI** excels at text rendering.

### How much will I spend per month?
Most designers spend $5-20 per month. Light users (under 100 images) typically spend under $5.

### Are there any hidden fees?
None from us. Provider pricing is published publicly. We add nothing on top.

### What if I run out of credits?
You never "run out" with BYOK. You pay per image as you go. Top up your provider account balance when needed.

### Can I use multiple providers?
Absolutely. Add API keys for all four providers and switch based on your task.
