## The Prompt Structure Formula

### Subject + Style + Details + Technical Specs

Every effective prompt follows this structure:

| Component | Purpose | Example |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Subject | What the image shows | "A golden retriever puppy" |
| Style | The artistic approach | "watercolor illustration" |
| Details | Specific attributes | "sitting in autumn leaves, warm sunlight" |
| Technical Specs | Output parameters | "soft focus background, 4K quality" |

**Basic prompt:** "A dog"

**Optimized prompt:** "A golden retriever puppy sitting in a pile of orange autumn leaves, watercolor illustration style, warm afternoon sunlight filtering through trees, playful curious expression, soft focus background"

---

## Component 1: Subject

### Define What Your Image Shows

Be specific, not generic:

| Weak Subject | Strong Subject |
|--------------|----------------|
| "A person" | "A woman in her 30s with curly red hair" |
| "A building" | "A Victorian-era brick townhouse" |
| "Food" | "A steaming bowl of ramen with soft-boiled egg" |

**Tips:**
1. Name the specific type ("German Shepherd" not "dog")
2. Include relevant context
3. Specify quantity when it matters
4. Consider the action ("dancer mid-leap" vs "dancer")

---

## Component 2: Style

### Set the Visual Language

**Style categories:**
- **Artistic Movements:** Impressionist, Art Nouveau, Pop Art, Minimalist
- **Photography Types:** Portrait, product, street, macro, aerial
- **Illustration Styles:** Watercolor, oil painting, digital art, vector
- **Era-Specific:** 1950s advertising, 80s synthwave, Victorian
- **Medium-Specific:** Film photography, Polaroid, 35mm film grain

**Example:**
- "A coffee cup, **flat vector illustration style**, clean lines"
- "A coffee cup, **hyperrealistic product photography**, studio lighting"
- "A coffee cup, **watercolor painting**, loose brushstrokes"

---

## Component 3: Details

### Add Specificity and Character

**Detail categories:**
- **Physical Attributes:** Colors, textures, materials, sizes
- **Environment:** Setting, time of day, weather, surroundings
- **Lighting:** Direction, quality, color temperature
- **Mood:** Emotional tone, energy level
- **Composition:** Positioning, perspective, depth

**Example:**
"A cat [subject] sleeping [action] on a velvet armchair [location], afternoon sunlight streaming through lace curtains [lighting], cozy Victorian parlor [environment], peaceful dreamy atmosphere [mood]"

---

## Component 4: Technical Specifications

### Control Output Quality

**Useful terms:**
- **Quality:** "High resolution," "4K," "detailed," "sharp focus"
- **Camera:** "Shot on Canon 5D," "85mm lens," "shallow depth of field"
- **Rendering:** "Photorealistic," "hyperdetailed," "cinematic"
- **Composition:** "Rule of thirds," "symmetrical," "centered"

## Model-Specific Prompting Tips

### FLUX Models
**Best for:** Photorealism, complex scenes
- Write prompts conversationally
- Detailed lighting descriptions work well
- Great at spatial relationships

**Example:** "A professional barista carefully pouring steamed milk into a ceramic cup, creating latte art, modern specialty coffee shop, morning light through large windows, shallow depth of field"

### GPT-Image 1.5
**Best for:** Text in images, conceptual imagery
- Specify exact text in quotes
- Strong at abstract concepts
- Handles layout requests well

**Example:** "A motivational poster with the text 'START TODAY' in bold sans-serif typography, sunrise over mountains, modern minimalist style"

### Nano Banana Pro
**Best for:** Artistic styles, creative interpretations
- Responds well to artistic style references
- Excellent color reproduction
- Good at stylized outputs

**Example:** "Whimsical illustration of a cozy bookshop cat sleeping on vintage books, Studio Ghibli inspired style, warm amber and sage green palette, soft watercolor textures"

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Being Too Vague
**Problem:** "A nice landscape"
**Fix:** "Rolling green hills under a dramatic sunset sky, golden hour lighting, scattered wildflowers, peaceful pastoral mood"

### Mistake 2: Contradictory Instructions
**Problem:** "A minimalist design with lots of intricate details"
**Fix:** Choose one direction

### Mistake 3: Overloading with Keywords
**Problem:** "Beautiful stunning amazing gorgeous incredible"
**Fix:** Use one quality descriptor, then add specific details

### Mistake 4: Ignoring Composition
**Problem:** "A mountain, a lake, trees, a cabin, birds, clouds, a person"
**Fix:** "A hiker on a trail in the foreground, overlooking a mountain lake with a cabin on the shore, framed by evergreen trees"

### Mistake 5: Forgetting Lighting
**Fix:** Always include lighting: "soft diffused light," "dramatic side lighting," "warm golden hour"

## Advanced Techniques

### Weighted Emphasis
Use explicit priority language:
"Most importantly, the lighting should be dramatic. The background is secondary."

### Negative Prompting
Tell the AI what you don't want:
"Professional headshot, natural expression, **without heavy makeup, no artificial skin smoothing, avoid overly posed positioning**"

### Style Fusion
Combine multiple references:
"Portrait photography **with Wes Anderson color grading and Annie Leibovitz composition**"

### Perspective Control
Be explicit about camera position:
- "**Bird's eye view:** breakfast table with dishes arranged in grid"
- "**Low angle looking up:** towering redwood trees"

### Aspect Ratio Optimization
- **1:1:** Social media, portraits
- **4:5:** Instagram posts
- **16:9:** Presentations, hero images
- **9:16:** Stories, mobile wallpapers

## FAQ Section

### How long should prompts be?
30-75 words typically works well. Too short yields generic results. Too long can confuse the model.

### Should I use complete sentences or keywords?
Complete sentences tend to produce better results for complex scenes. Blend both approaches.

### Why doesn't the AI include everything I describe?
Elements mentioned first or described most specifically appear more reliably. Move important elements earlier in your prompt.

### How do I get consistent results?
Use detailed, specific prompts. Save prompts that work well for reuse.

### Can I reference specific artists?
Describe the specific attributes you want rather than naming artists: "dramatic black and white portrait with high contrast and strong directional lighting."
