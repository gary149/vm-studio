## The Formula

### Subject + Style + Details + Technical Specs

| Part | What it does | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| Subject | What the image shows | "A golden retriever puppy" |
| Style | Artistic approach | "watercolor illustration" |
| Details | Specific attributes | "autumn leaves, warm sunlight" |
| Tech Specs | Output quality | "soft focus background, 4K" |

**Basic:** "A dog"

**Better:** "Golden retriever puppy in autumn leaves, watercolor style, warm sunlight, playful expression, soft focus background"

---

## Subject Tips

Be specific, not generic:

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "A person" | "Woman in her 30s, curly red hair" |
| "A building" | "Victorian brick townhouse" |
| "Food" | "Steaming ramen with soft-boiled egg" |

**Key tips:**
- Name the specific type
- Include context
- Add action when relevant

## Style Options

**Photography:** Portrait, product, street, macro, aerial

**Illustration:** Watercolor, vector, digital art, oil painting

**Era:** 1950s advertising, 80s synthwave, Victorian

**Medium:** Film photography, Polaroid, 35mm grain

Same subject, different styles:
- "Coffee cup, **flat vector**, clean lines"
- "Coffee cup, **product photography**, studio lighting"
- "Coffee cup, **watercolor**, loose brushstrokes"

## Common Mistakes

**Too vague**
Bad: "A nice landscape"
Good: "Rolling green hills, dramatic sunset, golden hour, scattered wildflowers"

**Contradictory**
Bad: "Minimalist with intricate details"
Good: Pick one direction

**Keyword stuffing**
Bad: "Beautiful stunning amazing gorgeous"
Good: One quality word + specific details

**No lighting**
Always include: "soft diffused light," "dramatic side lighting," "warm golden hour"

## Advanced Techniques

**Priority language**
"Most importantly, the lighting should be dramatic. Background is secondary."

**Negative prompting**
"Professional headshot, natural expression, **without heavy makeup, avoid stiff posing**"

**Style fusion**
"Portrait with **Wes Anderson colors** and **Leibovitz composition**"

**Aspect ratios**
- 1:1 — Social, portraits
- 16:9 — Presentations, heroes
- 9:16 — Stories, mobile

## FAQ

**How long should prompts be?**
30-75 words. Too short = generic. Too long = confusion.

**Why doesn't AI include everything?**
Elements mentioned first appear more reliably. Put important things early.

**How to get consistent results?**
Detailed prompts. Save what works. Reuse.
